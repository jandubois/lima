## Assessment

The failure rate rose to 3.1% from 2.3%, and failures on `master` to 24 from 10, but 7 of the 24 came from the VMNet regression that #5486 brought in W44, and that regression has stopped. The macOS VMNet job lost its runner on 7 `master` runs before #5729 (11-04 12:57Z) ran the host-network guest under TCG, and it has passed 18 of 18 `master` runs since. The flake to root-cause is still `vz-exfat-mount-after-mkfs`, which failed 14 of 97 vz `default.yaml` jobs this week (7 on `master`) against 11 of 98 in W44, so it has held at the rate it reached after the Ubuntu 26.10 update (#5657) instead of fading. Nothing in the logs says why the guest's first mount fails, and `bats::on_failure` in `hack/bats/extras/vz-block-device.bats` still doesn't capture the guest's `dmesg` or `blkid`, so adding that is the next step. The jobs that failed without a log rose to 52 from 22, and 27 of them came from the `vmnet-runner-loss`, `vmnet-two-vms` and `vmnet-vz-repeat` experiment workflows; all 8 on `master` lost their runner. The one regression candidate is the hdiutil eject failure that #5354's `-force` never fixed, because it covers only the unmount; it failed 2 PR jobs this week, and `TestChownIntegration` still detaches without a retry.

<details>
<summary>How this report is built</summary>

`ci-sweep weekly --week 2026-W45` over `sweep.db`, one row per **failed job** — a run can fail on one matrix leg out of many, so the job is the unit.

- **Known flake** — a job whose log matched a `matchers.yaml` pattern. Pattern matching only, no LLM.
- **One flake per job** — when several matchers fire, the job counts under the one whose surface appears **earliest** in the log, because the first failure is the root and what follows is fallout: a suite killed at its wall-clock cap logs a failed wait on the way out. The others are recorded but not counted, and only a true tie is left uncounted.
- **Proven flake** — the identical job passed on a re-run of the same commit, so the code cannot be at fault.
- **Unrecognized** — no matcher fired. On `master` it earns analysis; on a PR branch it earns only a count, because PR failures are dev breakage, not CI health.
- **No log** — the log expired or was never uploaded, so the sweep could not classify the failure. A data gap, not a clean bill.
- **What failed** — for a known flake, the matcher's summary; otherwise the failing test and command, or the job error for a failure that never reached its tests. `--fetch` names the rest, at one download each.
- Counts cover only jobs the sweep recorded. A period never swept reads as zero failures.

</details>
