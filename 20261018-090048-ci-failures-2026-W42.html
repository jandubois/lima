Assessment ¶

Two PR branches account for 172 of the 248 failed jobs, so the jump to 7.2% from 1.5% says little about master. fix/strict-type-definitions failed 125 jobs, 119 of them on its own compile and lint errors, and the gvisor-tap-vsock 0.9.0 Dependabot PR (#5616) failed 47, 28 of them on compile errors, before it merged with the code changes on 10-15. On master, 20 of the 33 failures were codeload.github.com answering 404 for action archives between 05:13 and 05:15Z on 10-15, and all 15 Dependabot jobs it hit in those minutes passed on re-run. Of the rest, hostagent-event-partial-line (4 jobs, 1 on master) is a lima bug, and it will keep failing an occasional limactl start until events.Watch waits for complete lines in the hostagent's event log. go-module-proxy-fetch-failed rose to 10 from 4, but five of those were fix/strict-type-definitions runs between 12:56 and 13:14 on 10-14, so I don't read it as a trend. BATS fails on every run now, because ftp-archive.freebsd.org still timed out at 08:52Z on 10-18 and both FreeBSD 15 image locations point there; the infra row on master is a 10-18 re-run that hit it, and the template needs another mirror unless the host comes back.

How this report is built

ci-sweep weekly --week 2026-W42 over sweep.db, one row per failed job — a run can fail on one matrix leg out of many, so the job is the unit.

  • Known flake — a job whose log matched a matchers.yaml pattern. Pattern matching only, no LLM.
  • One flake per job — when several matchers fire, the job counts under the one whose surface appears earliest in the log, because the first failure is the root and what follows is fallout: a suite killed at its wall-clock cap logs a failed wait on the way out. The others are recorded but not counted, and only a true tie is left uncounted.
  • Proven flake — the identical job passed on a re-run of the same commit, so the code cannot be at fault.
  • Unrecognized — no matcher fired. On master it earns analysis; on a PR branch it earns only a count, because PR failures are dev breakage, not CI health.
  • No log — the log expired or was never uploaded, so the sweep could not classify the failure. A data gap, not a clean bill.
  • What failed — for a known flake, the matcher's summary; otherwise the failing test and command, or the job error for a failure that never reached its tests. --fetch names the rest, at one download each.
  • Counts cover only jobs the sweep recorded. A period never swept reads as zero failures.
